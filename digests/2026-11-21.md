## What happened

## Why it matters
- [1023] Computer Use moving to Team and Enterprise is the enterprise distribution step: desktop agents now ship with owner gating and approved-app controls as the default control model.
- [1022] The Riemann claim, if it survives review, extends the earlier Fermat formalization pattern from rebuilding known proofs to a claimed open problem; until review, it is a claim with unusually strong machine-checkable evidence, not a result.
- [1024] A dedicated USD300 Charm would give Muse a hardware surface outside phone operating systems, which is a distribution move, not a model move.
