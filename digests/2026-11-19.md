# Agentic Radar — 2026-11-19

## What happened
- [997] Google Gemini 4 Neon went GA on Nov 18 at $3/$15 per million tokens with a 1M-token context, for coding, knowledge work and science; GitHub Copilot added it the same day. [announced-live]
- [998] Reuters reported on Nov 18 that Meta's Muse agent sent users messages seemingly meant for other accounts, surfaced bank balances that were not theirs, and referenced Signal messages, based on employee posts inside the company; Meta called the cases isolated incidents.
- [1009] Google made Tasks in Gemini free on Nov 17 in the US, Canada, Australia, India, Japan and Korea, on Gemini 4 Hydrogen, with US business calling and capped Google Pay purchases. [announced-live]
- [1000] OpenAI announced healthcare computer-use agents on Nov 16 via ChatGPT for Healthcare and the API; OpenAI reports 99% of 325 reviewed GPT-6 Astra EHR tasks rated clinically safe, with CVS and Cigna in production. [case-study]
- [1002] Codex CLI 0.169, released Nov 13, adds multiple side-chat tabs and task search, and makes codex exec keep project config and MCP servers disabled in untrusted directories unless --trust is passed.
- [1001] Claude Code v2.1.323, released Nov 12, adds experimental opt-in Code Mode (Claude writes JavaScript to call its tools), plugin version gating, and a 1M-token default for unrecognized model IDs.

## Why it matters
- [997] Google filled every Gemini 4 tier within a week — Hydrogen, Carbon, now Neon — giving builders a callable mid-tier today, not a preview.
- [998] The Muse incidents are the operational lesson: a consumer agent with deep app access can leak across accounts — a risk attaching to every comparable launch, including [1009].
- [1009] Free Tasks in six countries moves a personal agent from paid early access to mass distribution, with calling and payments as the differentiator.
